Return Policy
Customers can return a product within 7 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging with all accessories. To request a return, contact support with the order number. Products that were installed or used, such as an installed air conditioner, can only be returned if they are found defective. Once the returned product is received and checked, the refund is processed within 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method.

Refund Policy
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer. Delivery charges are not refunded unless the product was damaged or the wrong item was sent.

Shipping and Delivery
Delivery inside metro areas takes 1 to 3 business days. Delivery to other areas takes 3 to 5 business days. Delivery is free for orders above 200 US dollars. For orders below that amount, the delivery charge is 5 US dollars inside metro areas and 10 US dollars to other areas. Large items such as refrigerators and televisions are delivered by our own delivery team, who will call before arriving. Please check the product for damage in front of the delivery person before accepting it.

Installation
Free installation is included for air conditioners inside metro areas. In other areas, installation is arranged through the nearest authorized service center and may carry a charge. Installation for televisions and refrigerators is free of charge where a technician is required.

Warranty
Every product comes with the manufacturer warranty listed on its product page. Televisions carry 1 to 2 years of warranty depending on the brand. Refrigerators carry 1 to 2 years of full warranty, and the compressor has a longer warranty of 5 to 10 years depending on the brand. Air conditioners carry 1 to 3 years of full warranty and 5 years on the compressor for selected models. Warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage, or damage caused by unstable voltage. To claim warranty, keep the invoice and contact support with the order number.

Payment
We accept bank cards, online payment services such as PayPal, and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 1000 US dollars.

Order Cancellation
An order can be cancelled free of charge before it is shipped. After shipping, the customer must refuse delivery or follow the return policy.

Contact and Support
Support is available from 9 AM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday. For complaints, damaged products or cases the assistant cannot solve, customers can ask to be connected to a human support agent.
